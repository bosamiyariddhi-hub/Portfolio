About Me

I've always been drawn to understanding what makes a business tick — how a company generates revenue, what drives its margins, and whether the market price reflects its true worth. That curiosity is what led me to equity research.

During my internship at Infinite Financial Experts, I had the chance to dig into annual reports and financial statements across four sectors — FMCG, auto ancillary, specialty chemicals, and infrastructure. Evaluating companies like Dabur India, UNO Minda, and PNC Infratech taught me to look beyond headline numbers and understand the business behind them.

Clearing CFA Level I gave me a stronger conceptual foundation in corporate finance, financial reporting, and portfolio management. But what keeps me going is the learning itself — reading earnings transcripts, building models in Excel, and forming a view on where an industry is headed.

My goal is straightforward: to build a career in equity research where I can contribute meaningful investment analysis. I want to be in a role where rigorous thinking, attention to detail, and intellectual curiosity are the core requirements.
